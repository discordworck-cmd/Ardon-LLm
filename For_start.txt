Ardon’s unique properties allow you to train a model in just a single pass!
Use this to generate text, music, and more—even when running on a CPU.

Steps:
Everything you need to train the model is provided. Simply:

1. Insert your key.
2. Create a folder named "my_data".
3. Download about 100 MB worth of books and place them into that folder.
4. Run `Ardon_train.py`.
5. Run `Ardon_Chat.py`.

Training takes approximately 4 hours on a CPU, or 1 hour on a GPU.
